III The Artist

Inidė Jasnauskaitė

b. Lithuania. Lives and works in Vilnius.

Works in fashion, beauty, and lifestyle. Her pieces reach an audience of 359,000 collectors daily. Selected commissions: Mionetto, Hellmann's, Nissan, Akropolis, Maxima, McDonald's, Bioderma, MyProtein, and sixty-five others. Her practice encompasses both long-form brand narratives and organic cultural observation — the latter often outperforming the former.
