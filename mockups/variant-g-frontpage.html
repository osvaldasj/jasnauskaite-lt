Lithuanian Creator Reaches 590 Million Views; Brands Keep Coming Back

Eight years, 69 partners, 787 branded posts — an audit of an unusually consistent track record in an industry where most accounts peak and retreat in eighteen months.

By Reelize Staff  ·  Vilnius, June 12

VILNIUS — A social media portfolio reviewed by this newspaper this week suggests that Inidė Jasnauskaitė, the fashion and lifestyle content creator who has published on Instagram and TikTok since 2016, has accumulated an audience and commercial relationship record that is, by most measurable standards, unusual for the Lithuanian market.

The figures are plain enough: 359 000 Instagram followers, a 5.0 per cent engagement rate at a moment when the platform average sits closer to 1.9, and a cumulative view count now exceeding 590 million. The more telling detail is the repeat-client rate. Of 69 brands that have commissioned content, a substantial proportion have returned for second and third campaigns — a data point that is difficult to manufacture and that agencies, in private, regard as the only metric that truly cannot be faked.

"You can buy followers," one brand manager told this newspaper on condition of anonymity. "You cannot, in eight years, buy back repeat bookings from the likes of Mionetto and Hellmann's."

The Mionetto campaign — a prosecco brand positioning exercise covering the spring drinking occasion — drew 16.7 million views. Hellmann's, the condiment manufacturer whose Lithuanian operation commissioned a single recipe reel, saw that post accrue 14 million views before the campaign window closed. Neither figure required a paid-amplification budget.

Akropolis Group, the shopping-centre operator with properties in four Baltic cities, ran a 35-post season contract yielding 13.2 million combined views. Nissan Lithuania executed a multi-part automotive series that closed at 6.5 million. In each case, the content category — fashion, beauty, home, food — was treated not as product placement but as editorially coherent storytelling, a distinction that audiences, apparently, notice.

Food · Brand Partnerships

Mayonnaise Reel Tops 14 Million

A single recipe video commissioned by Hellmann's Lithuania — in which a jar of mayonnaise is deployed with improbable elegance in a pasta preparation — has become the most-viewed piece of branded food content produced by a Lithuanian creator in the past twenty-four months. The result surprised the brand's regional marketing team, who had budgeted conservatively. A repeat contract is understood to have been offered.

Retail · Long-Form Contracts

Mall Giant Books 35-Post Season; Views Hit 13.2M

Akropolis Group, operator of the largest retail complexes in Vilnius, Kaunas, Riga and Tallinn, signed a season-length content agreement with Jasnauskaitė for a fashion and lifestyle series to run across autumn and winter. The thirty-five posts, combining outfit content, brand spotlights, and editorial-style lookbooks, reached a combined 13.2 million views. Industry observers note the contract length as an indicator of institutional confidence unusual in short-form creator agreements.

Automotive · Series Format

On the Road: Nissan Series Closes at 6.5M

Nissan Lithuania's multi-episode content series — structured around travel and lifestyle rather than technical specification — represents a format that has proven effective for automotive brands seeking to reach audiences who do not self-identify as car enthusiasts. The six-post arc, filmed across three Lithuanian locations, closed at 6.5 million views. The project illustrates a recurring pattern in Jasnauskaitė’s commercial work: high-production content that sits comfortably alongside organic posts without triggering the instinctive disengagement audiences direct at obviously promotional material.
